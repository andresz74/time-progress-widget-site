# Time Progress Widget Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Time Progress Widget does not collect personal data through the app or widget. It does not require an account, show ads, use third-party analytics, or track you across apps or websites.

The app uses your device’s date, time, and regional settings to calculate progress for the current day, week, month, and year. Font, appearance, and row order preferences are stored on your device and shared locally between the app and widget. These preferences are not sent to the developer. You can remove the app and its local settings by deleting the app.

If you email support, we use the information you choose to share to respond to your request. For privacy questions or to request deletion of a support message, email [azenteno74@gmail.com](mailto:azenteno74@gmail.com).
