# Time Progress Widget Support

Time Progress shows how much of the current day, week, month, and year has passed.

## Add the widget

Open Time Progress once, then touch and hold an empty area of your iPhone Home Screen. Choose **Edit → Add Widget**, search for **Time Progress**, and add the medium widget. In the app, you can choose a font and appearance and change the order of the rows.
